## Safety (AC-6)
- **Subprocess audit:** every row made only 3 subprocess calls, all `git -C <root>` `remote` / `log` / `for-each-ref`. The harness patches `subprocess.run` and fails on anything else.
- **No writes:** the scanned repos' top-level entries were unchanged after each `--stdout` scan. No `.workflow/` was written into any private repo.
- **Hard timeout:** `SIGALRM` at `2 × budget + 2` seconds stops pathological repos. Before this was added, a catastrophic-backtracking regex in CI parsing caused a hang; it was replaced with a line scan.
